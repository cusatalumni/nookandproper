About us

Last updated 2026-10-22

Nook & Proper is a small independent edit of practical things for the home — the kind of purchase that makes an everyday job slightly easier rather than the kind you admire in a photograph and never use.

What we cover

Five categories, chosen because the sales data says people are actually buying them: home organisation, pet enrichment, the air-fryer kitchen, home fitness, and sleep. We deliberately do not cover beauty or fashion.

How we choose

We start from marketplace demand — what is genuinely selling, not what looks good on a mood board. Each pick then has to pass a simpler test: would we explain it to a friend without embarrassment. If a product only makes sense as an advert, it does not go in.

How we make money

Through affiliate commissions. If you buy through a link on this site we may earn a small percentage, at no extra cost to you. That is the whole business model — we do not sell your data and we do not take payment for a placement. See our affiliate disclosure for the detail.

Corrections

Prices and availability change constantly. If something here is wrong, tell us and we will fix it rather than quietly leave it up.
