Wake Up Before Your Alarm Does

2026-10-24 · Sunrise alarms · $30–60

A sunrise alarm works on the part of waking that an alarm cannot touch: the light your body expects before it is asked to move. Twenty minutes of gradual brightening does more for how mornings feel than a louder sound or a later alarm. The specification to check is minimum brightness — a lamp that starts too bright is just an alarm that lights up.
