The Bed That Calms an Anxious Dog

2026-10-22 · Calming dog beds · $25–50

The chin-up rim is the whole design. Dogs that rest their head on an edge settle faster because the raised boundary reads as cover, which is why the donut shape outperforms a flat pad for anxious animals. Medium suits most spaniels and terriers. Make sure the cover comes off for washing — a calming bed that cannot be washed stops being calming in about ten days.
